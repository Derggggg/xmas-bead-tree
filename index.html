How it's made

Five steps. Two cosy evenings.

Every kit comes with printed step cards and a video. Here is the whole journey, from a box of beads to a glowing tree. Read the full guide →

  1. 0110 min

    Sort

    Pour the beads into the tray, one colour per compartment. A perfect job for the youngest helper.

  2. 0260–90 min

    Thread

    Slide beads onto each pre-cut wire, following the colour pattern on the card. Each wire becomes one sparkling branch.

  3. 0320 min

    Shape

    Fold each beaded wire into a loop and give it a gentle twist, so it fans out like a pine branch.

  4. 0430 min

    Build

    Twist the branches onto the centre stem, longest at the bottom, weaving the light string through as you go.

  5. 051 min

    Glow

    Crown it with the pearl angel, flip the switch in the base, and turn off the big lights.

Questions, answered

Do I need any experience?

No. If you can thread a bead, you can make this. The printed cards, the online guide and the video walk you through every step.

How long does it take?

About 2–3 hrs in total. Most people spread it over two relaxed evenings.

Is it suitable for children?

Children aged 10 and up can build it with a little help. The beads are small, so keep the kit away from children under 3.

What powers the lights?

A battery box in the base: 2 × AA batteries.

How fast is delivery?

We ship from Singapore. Orders placed by 2 pm on a working day go out the same day and usually arrive in 1–2 working days. See shipping & returns.

How big is the finished tree?

About 20 cm tall, a good size for a desk, shelf or bedside table.

Something is missing or broken. What now?

Email us with your order number and a photo, and we will send the missing part or a replacement. See returns.
