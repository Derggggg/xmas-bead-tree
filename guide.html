Making guide

How to make your bead tree

Five steps. Total time: About 2–3 hrs. Take it slowly; most people spread it over two evenings. This page matches the printed cards in your box.

Before you start1 · Sort2 · Thread3 · Shape4 · Build5 · GlowTroubleshooting

Before you start

  • Find a flat table with good light. Lay down a tea towel so stray beads don't roll away.
  • Check the contents against the list on card 1. Tell us within 7 days if anything is missing.
  • Keep beads and wire ends away from children under 3 and from pets.
  • Put on a playlist and make a warm drink. Highly recommended.
0110 min

Sort

  1. Open each bead bag and pour it into its own compartment of the tray, one colour per section.
  2. Keep the spare beads bag aside. You will use it if a few go missing.

Tip: this is the perfect job for the youngest helper.

0260–90 min

Thread the branches

  1. Take one pre-cut branch wire. Bend a tiny hook at one end so beads can't slide off.
  2. Thread beads following the colour pattern on your card. Leave about 3 cm of bare wire at the end.
  3. Sort finished branches into groups by length: long, medium and short.

Tip: scoop beads with the tip of the wire straight from the tray instead of picking them up one by one. It's much faster.

0320 min

Shape

  1. Fold each beaded wire into a loop and twist the two bare ends together two or three turns.
  2. Gently fan the loop open so it looks like a little pine branch.

Tip: twist firmly but don't over-tighten, or the wire may snap.

0430 min

Build the tree

  1. Fix the centre stem into the base.
  2. Starting from the bottom, twist the longest branches around the stem in a ring, then work upward with medium and short branches.
  3. As you go, spiral the light string up the stem and tuck the bulbs between the branches so they shine from inside.
  4. Leave the top 2 cm of stem free for the angel.

Tip: turn the tree as you work so it looks full from every side.

051 min

Glow

  1. Place the pearl angel on top.
  2. Insert the batteries into the box in the base and flip the switch.
  3. Turn off the big lights. Take a photo, and tag us if you like.

Troubleshooting

Beads keep sliding off the wire

Re-bend the hook at the end of the wire a little tighter.

Branches droop

Twist the bare ends a few more turns around the stem, or double up two short branches.

The lights don't turn on

Check the batteries are the right way round and the switch is fully on. Still nothing? Email us and we'll send a replacement light string.

Something is missing

Email us with your order number and a photo, and we'll post it to you.

Looking after your tree

  • Dust with a soft, dry brush.
  • Take the batteries out after the season and store the tree in its original box.
