The small print

Shipping, returns & privacy

Shipping

  • We deliver within Singapore only.
  • Orders placed by 2 pm on a working day are dispatched the same day and usually arrive in 1–2 working days.
  • Delivery is S$4, and free on orders over S$60.
  • For delivery before Christmas, please order by 20 Dec.

Returns & replacements

  • Missing or faulty parts: email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send the part or a replacement kit at no cost.
  • Change of mind: unopened kits can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Return postage is paid by the customer.
  • Opened kits can't be returned for change of mind, as beads are difficult to re-count and re-pack.

Privacy

We collect your name, phone number, email and delivery address only to process and deliver your order and to contact you about it. Payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your full card details. We do not sell your data. You can ask us to see or delete your personal data at any time by emailing us.
